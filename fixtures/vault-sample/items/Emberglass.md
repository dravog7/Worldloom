# Emberglass

Warm-to-the-touch glass that forms where the [[Emberwood]] fires meet
water. Lamps made from it never go out; swords made from it never quite
stop burning.

The [[Lantern Guild]] trades it, [[Brannoc]] ferries it, and the
kiln-masters who gather it do so on the night-shift only, which nobody
thinks is a coincidence after [[The Night of Cinders]].

Its forge-chemistry appears nowhere in the corpus of [[The Ashen Choir]],
which is the single fact [[Session 05 - The Choir Unmasked]] turns on.
