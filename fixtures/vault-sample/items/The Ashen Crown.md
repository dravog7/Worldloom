# The Ashen Crown

A circlet of grey metal that never cools. Worn by [[Halden II]] since the
[[Coronation of Halden]]; claimed, with paperwork, by [[Aelindra]];
claimed, with songs, by [[The Ashen Choir]].

The metallurgists of [[Vael'Thor]] refuse to describe its alloy. The
kiln-masters of [[Emberwood]] would recognize it instantly — it is the
same glass as [[Emberglass]], and it is why the Guild pays cash.
