# Session 03 — Politics of Vael'Thor

An audience nobody survived gracefully: [[Halden II]] received them like
a man receiving weather, and [[Sereth Vane]] watched from the colonnade
with her famous eyes on every face at the table.

The [[Iron Compact|Compact's]] captains drunk-bragged the [[Treaty of the Shattered Span]] toll rates into existence. [[Aelindra]] was not in
the room; her name was, three times.

Hook planted: the palace archives hold a coronation witness-list with one
line scraped out.
