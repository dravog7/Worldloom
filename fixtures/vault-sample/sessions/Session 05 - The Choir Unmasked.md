# Session 05 — The Choir Unmasked

The kiln-vault under [[Emberwood]] held the tithe-ledger of
[[The Ashen Choir]]: nine years of [[Bloodmagic]] debts, priced in
[[Emberglass]] and signed in ash.

[[Kestrel]] burned two pages. [[Sister Wren]] read the rest aloud. The
Wardens' oldest warden finally said the word "Sundering" out loud.

Hook planted: the ledger's last entry is dated the week before the
[[The Sundering|Sundering]] — in a hand that has not changed in a
thousand years.
