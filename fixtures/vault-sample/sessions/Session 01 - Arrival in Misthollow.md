# Session 01 — Arrival in Misthollow

The party made port in [[Misthollow]] in weather the sailors call
"polite fog." [[Mira]] fed them; [[Brannoc]] ferried them; the Guild's
factor watched all of it and wrote his weekly report.

Loot: one sealed letter in a dead man's coat, addressed to a name
nobody in the party recognized.

Hook planted: the letter's seal is the [[Order of the Pale Hand]].
