# Session 02 — The Emberwood Road

Three days under the warm rain of embers with [[Kestrel]], who told them
the fire-road obeys the [[Wardens of the Ember]] and nothing else, and
demonstrated by walking them past a warding-stone the hard way.

Found: a kiln village, cold for years, with one warm room. Kestrel would
not name what the room remembered of [[The Night of Cinders]].

Hook planted: the kiln-masters' debt-book mentions "the Choir's glass."
