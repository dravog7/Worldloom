# Session 04 — The Shattered Span

[[Brannoc]] ferried the party across at low water while both patrols
pretended the fog was thicker than it was. Mid-span, they counted
[[Iron Compact]] banners: forty tents where the [[Treaty of the Shattered Span]] allows twelve.

Found: fresh rope-burn on the old arch, someone climbing the sealed side.

Hook planted: whoever maintains the sealed stair pays in Guild glass.
