# Misthollow

A fog-bound fishing village on the grey water, where the [[Lantern Guild]] keeps a pier and [[Mira]] keeps a tavern. The fog is honest here:
it hides everyone equally.

The party landed in [[Session 01 - Arrival in Misthollow]] and left on
[[Brannoc]]'s boat, which the village counted as a festival. The Guild's
factor in Misthollow still writes weekly reports to [[Vael'Thor]] about
nothing at all.
