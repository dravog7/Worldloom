# The Shattered Span

The bridge that the [[The Sundering|Sundering]] broke and eleven years of
diplomacy did not: half its arches stand in the river, and what passes for
traffic now goes by [[Brannoc]]'s ferries.

The [[Treaty of the Shattered Span]] hangs its name on this ruin. Both
sides patrol their half; both halves are watched by the other's spies.
Crossing it in [[Session 04 - The Shattered Span]] is where the party
first saw the [[Iron Compact]]'s true numbers.
