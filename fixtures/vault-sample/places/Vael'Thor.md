# Vael'Thor

The ash-grey capital, built in terraces above the slow river, where the
bells ring for kings living and dead. Seat of [[Halden II]], grave of the
Vael line, hunger of the [[Iron Compact]].

Every road in the realm ends here: the fog road from [[Misthollow]], the
ember road from [[Emberwood]], the treaty road across [[The Shattered Span]]. [[Aelindra]] was born in the palace and declared dead in its
cathedral — see [[Coronation of Halden]].

[[Commander Aldous]] commands its walls; [[Sereth Vane]] commands its
silences. In the year of the [[Siege of Vael'Thor]], both were tested.
