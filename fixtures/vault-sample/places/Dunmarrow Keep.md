# Dunmarrow Keep

A black basalt keep above the ember-road, garrisoned by the [[Iron Compact]] and haunted, per every soldier who has slept in it, by exactly
one cold room nobody can find twice.

[[Sister Wren]] preached in its yard for a season. The keep's masons
swear the foundations predate the [[The Sundering|Sundering]] and match
nothing else in the realm — least of all the sealed stair the [[Vault of Whispers]] legend hangs on.
