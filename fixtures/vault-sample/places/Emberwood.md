# Emberwood

A burning forest that never goes out and never grows: embers drift from
the canopy like snow, warm on the hand. The [[Wardens of the Ember]] keep
its fire-road; nothing else in it is kept.

The [[Session 02 - The Emberwood Road|party]] crossed it under
[[Kestrel]]'s guidance. Old charcoal-kilns dot the southern edge, and the
kiln-masters remember [[The Night of Cinders]] better than they would like.

[[Brannoc]] buys the wood's one export — [[Emberglass]] — at prices the
Wardens call theft and he calls logistics.
