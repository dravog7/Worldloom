# The Night of Cinders

The night the southern kilns of [[Emberwood]] burned for nine hours and
left no ash — only glass. The Wardens call it arson; the kiln-masters
call it a debt being collected; [[Sereth Vane]] calls it nothing at all,
which is how everyone learned it mattered.

The Guild bought the glass at dawn (see [[Emberglass]]). The Choir sang
about it before it happened (see [[The Ashen Choir]]).

Its ledger — the one [[Session 05 - The Choir Unmasked]] found — is the
only document that calls it by its true name: a [[Bloodmagic]] tithe.
