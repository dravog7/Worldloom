# Siege of Vael'Thor

Eleven days the gates held: [[Commander Aldous]] on the walls, [[Halden II]] in the crypt, the [[Iron Compact]] billing both. What the besiegers
wanted was never the city — it was the head wearing [[The Ashen Crown]].

[[Session 06 - Siege Prelude]] ends the day the first fireship cleared
the river bend. The bells of [[Vael'Thor]] rang through it all, for once
without being told to.
