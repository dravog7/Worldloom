# Coronation of Halden

The morning the bells of [[Vael'Thor]] rang for a living king and a dead
princess: [[Halden II]] took [[The Ashen Crown]] while the cathedral read
the death-record of [[Aelindra]] aloud, twice, for the witnesses.

Loyalists remember the exact wording. [[Sereth Vane]] wrote it.

Every act of the realm since — the Compact's charter, the Guild's rates,
the exile's long walk north — counts this date as year zero.
