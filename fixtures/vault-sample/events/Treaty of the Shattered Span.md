# Treaty of the Shattered Span

The peace nobody loves and everybody keeps: eleven years of ferry rights,
toll rates, and patrol rotations across the [[The Shattered Span|Span]],
guaranteed by the [[Iron Compact]] as a paid third party.

[[Brannoc]] owes his letters of marque to its clause seven. [[Commander Aldous]] owes his career to its clause eleven. Both have read clause
fourteen, the one about the sealed stair, and neither discusses it.

Ratified in the ruins it names, in the same month as [[Session 04 - The Shattered Span|the party's crossing]].
