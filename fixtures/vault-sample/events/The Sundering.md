# The Sundering

The event that broke the span, the wards, and the old calendar. No two
accounts agree on what it was; every account agrees on what it did.

The [[Wardens of the Ember]] formed the year after. [[Sister Wren]] walked
its road as a child and counts the years backwards since. The scholars of
[[Vael'Thor]] call it an earthquake and avoid [[The Loom of Fates]] in
their footnotes.

Its epicenter has never been found, which is the entire subject of
[[Session 05 - The Choir Unmasked]].
