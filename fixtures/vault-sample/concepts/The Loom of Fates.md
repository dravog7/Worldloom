# The Loom of Fates

The heresy that fate is woven, not granted: every thread answerable to
every other, no god holding the shuttle. Banned in [[Vael'Thor]] since
the [[Coronation of Halden]], whose priests preferred their fate
read-only.

[[Sister Wren]] keeps its roadside shrines. [[The Sundering]] is the
Loom's great unraveling in its texts — and its re-weaving, which the
orthodox consider worse. The [[The Ashen Choir|Choir]] sings a
counter-hymn that uses the same three notes.

[[Sereth Vane]] reads her mother's sayings from it, and denies it twice.
