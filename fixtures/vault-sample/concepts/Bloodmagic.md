# Bloodmagic

Payment magic: power extracted from a willing ledger of lives rather than
a spellbook. The realm banned the word after the [[The Sundering|Sundering]];
[[The Ashen Choir]] uses it as an accounting term.

The tithe records of [[The Night of Cinders]] are bloodmagic in this
sense — and the only kind that survives contact with the [[Iron Compact]]'s auditors. [[Emberglass]] is its byproduct, which is why
every glass-lamp in the realm is faintly warm.

The Choir denies it. [[Sereth Vane]] has the ledgers. [[Session 05 - The Choir Unmasked]] has the receipts.
