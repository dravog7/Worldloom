# The Ashen Choir

A robed congregation that sings the old funerary rites a little too well
and burns a little too brightly. They claim [[The Ashen Crown]] was never
meant for a living head.

[[Sereth Vane]] has spent three years failing to name a single canon of
the Choir. What [[Session 05 - The Choir Unmasked]] uncovered instead was
worse: a ledger of [[Bloodmagic]] debts owed in the embers of [[Emberwood]].

They hold that the fire in the forest is a choir in rehearsal.
