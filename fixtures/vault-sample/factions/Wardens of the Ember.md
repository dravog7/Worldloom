# Wardens of the Ember

Keepers of the fire-road through [[Emberwood]] since the [[The Sundering|Sundering]] broke the old wards. They count time by fires and
travel light: [[Kestrel]] is their whole diplomatic corps.

They trade little and answer less, except to [[Sister Wren]], whose
roadside shrines they quietly maintain against the priests of [[Vael'Thor]].

The charcoal-kilns pay them in embers, and the kiln-masters of the
[[Night of Cinders|Cinders night]] still owe them an explanation.
