# Iron Compact

A mercenary charter grown into a shadow estate: it garrisons [[Dunmarrow Keep]], funds [[Vael'Thor]], and negotiates its own treaties — see
[[Treaty of the Shattered Span]].

[[Commander Aldous]] wears its iron ring and means to return it. Its
companies drilled through the [[Siege of Vael'Thor]] and billed the crown
for every hour.

Its captains drink in [[Session 03 - Politics of Vael'Thor|the capital]]
and its sergeants freeze on the [[The Shattered Span|Span]].
