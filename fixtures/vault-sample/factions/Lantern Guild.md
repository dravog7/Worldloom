# Lantern Guild

Merchants of lamplight and glass: the [[Lantern Guild]] lights half the
realm and reads the other half for [[Sereth Vane]] as a sideline it calls
networking.

[[Brannoc]] sails under its letters of marque; [[Misthollow]] hosts its
pier; its caravans carry [[Emberglass]] with a customs rate that changes
depending on who is asking.

It has funded every side of every war since the [[The Sundering|Sundering]]
and considers this a moral position.
