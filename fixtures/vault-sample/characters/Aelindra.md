# Aelindra

Aelindra Vael, called **The Exile**, is the deposed heir of [[Vael'Thor]].
Her **grey-green** eyes — her father's eyes — are the mark every loyalist
remembers, and the reason she keeps her hood low.

Declared dead at the [[Coronation of Halden]], she walked out of the city
with nothing but a name. She keeps it hidden in [[Session 03 - Politics of Vael'Thor]], and her claim still burns on the brow of [[The Ashen Crown]].

Traveling with [[Kestrel]] since the Emberwood road, she is hunted by
[[Sereth Vane]] and watched by the [[Order of the Pale Hand]], whose
letters she burns unread.

She swears by [[The Loom of Fates]] that she will see [[Halden II]] answer
for the [[Night of Cinders|The Night of Cinders]].
