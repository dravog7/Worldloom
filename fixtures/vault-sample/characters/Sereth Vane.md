# Sereth Vane

Sereth Vane, the **Grey Lens**, reads the city the way scribes read
manuscripts — line by line, secret by secret. She reports only to
[[Halden II]].

Her agents wear the hoods of the [[Lantern Guild]]; her failures gather
in the cells beneath [[Vael'Thor]]. Those who have seen her unmasked note
her **storm-blue** eyes, a curious contrast to her father's famous grey.

She watched [[Aelindra]] grow up in the palace and has never been heard
to say whether the [[Coronation of Halden]] grieved her. What she did
afterward — the ledgers of [[The Night of Cinders]] — is another matter.

She reads the future in [[The Loom of Fates]] the way the [[The Ashen Choir|Ashen Choir]] does, and denies it with equal heat.
