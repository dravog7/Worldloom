# Commander Aldous

Aldous holds the walls of [[Vael'Thor]] and the leash of the [[Iron Compact|Iron Compact]] mercenaries. He taught half the city guard to
read, which the court considers a security risk and the guard considers
a promise.

He kept the terms of the [[Treaty of the Shattered Span]] to the letter
for eleven years, and broke the letter once — the night of the [[Siege of Vael'Thor]] — to open the sewer gates.

He served [[Halden II]] longer than he served the Vael crown and regrets
the arithmetic.
