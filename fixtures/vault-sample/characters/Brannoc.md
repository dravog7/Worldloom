# Brannoc

Brannoc Tidecrow runs cargo the way other people run errands — [[Misthollow]]
in the fog, [[The Shattered Span]] when the span-wrights allow it, and
anywhere the [[Lantern Guild]] pays.

His hold has carried [[Emberglass]] legally at least twice. He claims the
Treaty of the Shattered Span made him an honest man; the customs office of
[[Vael'Thor]] keeps a thicker file than that.

He ferried [[Session 01 - Arrival in Misthollow|the party]] out of
Misthollow in a storm and has been half-regretting it ever since.
