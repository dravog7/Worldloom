# Mira

Mira keeps the only tavern in [[Misthollow]] with a dry cellar and a
dryer sense of humor. She trades in rumors the [[Lantern Guild]] trades
in glass, and she is better at remembering faces.

She still wears her mother's locket — see [[Mira's Locket]], which she
refuses to sell to the collectors from [[Vael'Thor]] who keep asking.

[[Kestrel]] owes her four meals and one silence. She called in the silence
during [[Session 01 - Arrival in Misthollow]].
