# Sister Wren

Sister Wren tends the roadside shrines of [[The Loom of Fates]], the
weaving-fate heresy that the priesthood of [[Vael'Thor]] keeps meaning to
ban.

She walked the length of the [[The Sundering|Sundering]] road as a child
and still counts the years backwards. The Wardens hide nothing from her;
she hides a great deal from them.

At [[Dunmarrow Keep]] she preached to prisoners and gaolers in the same
hour, and both asked her to stay.
