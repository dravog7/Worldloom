# Halden II

Halden holds [[Vael'Thor]] by right of crown and by the strength of the
[[Iron Compact]]. His investiture — the [[Coronation of Halden]] — was
meant to bury the Vael line; instead it made a martyr of [[Aelindra]].

His spymaster [[Sereth Vane]] keeps him informed, his commander
[[Commander Aldous]] keeps him obeyed. The city bells still ring for the
old king, which is why he has thought about melting down
[[The Ashen Crown]] more than once.

When the [[Siege of Vael'Thor]] began, Halden refused the counsel of
retreat and held the gates with three hundred of the Compact's best.
