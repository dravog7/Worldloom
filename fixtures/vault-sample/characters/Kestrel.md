# Kestrel

Kestrel guides the [[Emberwood]] the way ferrymen guide rivers: by feel,
by lore, and by the old warding-stones the [[Wardens of the Ember]] still
maintain.

She met [[Aelindra]] on the [[Session 02 - The Emberwood Road|Emberwood road]] and has not left her side since, which everyone including [[Mira]]
has opinions about.

Her order remembers the [[The Sundering|Sundering]] in bone-lore rather
than ink, and does not discuss it with [[Sister Wren]].
